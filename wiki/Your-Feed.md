# Your Feed

Your feed shows activities from the people you follow. It's how you stay connected with your training community.

## Personal Feed

### What Appears

- Activities from users you follow
- Sorted by time (newest first)
- Includes public and followers-only activities

### Accessing Your Feed

1. Click **Feed** in the nav bar
2. See activities from people you follow
3. Click any activity to view details

### Feed Items Show

- Athlete name and profile link
- Activity title
- Activity type (ride, run, swim)
- Date and time
- Distance and duration
- Like count

## Public Feed

### What Appears

- All public activities from any user
- Sorted by time (newest first)
- No authentication required

### Accessing the Public Feed

1. Go to the Lièvre homepage (when logged out)
2. See recent public activities
3. Click any activity to view details

### When to Use

- Discover new athletes to follow
- See what's happening on the instance
- Browse without an account

## Feed Filters

### By Activity Type

Currently, the feed shows all activity types. Filtering by type (rides only, runs only) is planned for a future release.

### By Date

The feed is sorted by time — most recent first. Scroll down to see older activities.

## Activity Cards

Each activity in the feed shows:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  🚴 athlete · Morning Ride    8/14/2026 │
│     ride · 42.0 km · 1h 30m · ♥ 3      │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Athlete** — Click to view profile
- **Title** — Click to view activity
- **Stats** — Distance, duration, likes
- **Date** — When the activity started

## Feed Performance

- Activities load progressively (paginated)
- Initial load shows the most recent activities
- Scrolling loads more activities
- No infinite scroll — explicit "Load More" button

## Federated Activities

Activities from remote instances appear in your feed if:

1. You follow a remote user
2. The activity is public or followers-only
3. The remote instance supports federation

Remote activities show the same card format as local activities.
